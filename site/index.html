CouchElephant

A CouchElephant never forgets. It turns your Plex DVR into a full-featured DVR.

An add-on for Plex: the live airing, not the repeat, team and season passes, its own recorder with ad skipping, and live TV on your Apple TV, iPhone and iPad.

Download CouchElephant

Other systems

    Linux, one line

    On Debian, Ubuntu, Fedora, openSUSE or anything else with systemd:

    curl -fsSL https://couchelephant.app/install.sh | sudo sh

    It picks the right package for your system and your processor.

    Docker

    Any machine that can reach your Plex server:

    docker run -d --name couchelephant --restart unless-stopped \
  -p 8710:8710 \
  -v /opt/couchelephant/data:/data \
  -e TZ=UTC \
  ghcr.io/datbird/couchelephant:latest

    Unraid

    Search for CouchElephant in Apps.
